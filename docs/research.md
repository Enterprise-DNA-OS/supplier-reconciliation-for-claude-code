# Selection evidence

AP Accurate, supplier-statement-reconciliation family. Checked 30 October 2026. No existing target in this family was found in the Command Centre registry.

Annual bill score 4, data-shaped 5, buyer fit 2, search demand 1, one-session buildable 5, import path 3. Total 20/30. The upper indicative monthly price annualises to US$60,000 before rebates. This is a list-price scenario, not an observed NZ/AU customer invoice. No named subscriber or search volume was verified. Local customer materials did not establish AP Accurate usage.

[Vendor pricing](https://360-ap.com/pricing) lists AP Accurate at US$300 to US$5,000 per month, subject to volume, configuration and final quote. The same page offers rebates and multi-module discounts. [Product documentation](https://360-ap.com/ap-accurate) describes original CSV statement intake, matching rules, exceptions and reports. [Integrations](https://360-ap.com/integrations) describes accounting-system inputs. There is no public proprietary export schema, so this build imports the original supplier and ledger records with explicit column mapping. Proprietary matching history needs separate migration.

Candidates: AP Accurate 20/30 selected. ReturnGO 18/30 rejected because public entry prices did not establish a five-figure annual bill. AuditFile 15/30 rejected because external-audit responsibilities and export uncertainty exceed this build. AssetAccountant's small-volume pricing did not establish a qualifying bill. Supplier.io depends on a proprietary supplier network, outside the rebuild thesis.

Sources for those exclusions: https://returngo.ai/pricing/ , https://auditfile.com/pricing , https://www.asset.accountant/pricing-accounting-firms/ , https://supplier.io/pricing . All reopened this run. These are selection notes, not comparison claims on the page.

The scope deliberately leaves out payments, tax filing and assurance opinions. Its useful standalone work is source comparison and documented exception review. The chosen build is distinct from purchasing approvals, expense claims and general financial close.
